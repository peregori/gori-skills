# Outbound plays and related GTM ideas

A play connects a suitable audience and observed situation to a useful offer, message, and measurable next step. Use the project's ICP and research evidence. A clever opening without product fit or a useful offer is incomplete.

## Prepare a play for review

Keep plays in `ideas.md`, distinguished from social ideas by type. Each contains:

```text
ID and type: outbound play | content | ad concept | directory | community
Segment and ICP rule version
Problem and current workaround
Trigger: observed situation, sources/dates, freshness; or explicitly no trigger
Eligibility and exclusions; eligible sample references when available
Offer and useful next step; product/offer proof and unresolved assumptions
Message angle and why the recipient would care
Channel and how this audience can be reached there
Founder effort and estimated cash cost, with basis or unknown
Primary outcome, measurement source, denominator and review window
Decision rule: continue | revise | stop | inconclusive
Founder decision and actual execution status
```

Recommend the strongest few plays within the founder's time and budget. Show why others were deferred. Do not fill every channel or assign unsupported numerical conversion targets. Distinguish a proposed experiment from a selected play and from an actually launched campaign.

An unresolved offer need not block ICP research or angle discovery. Mark copy dependent on price, trial, discount, capacity, platform support, or launch readiness as incomplete. Do not borrow a video's credit incentive or invent a discount. Validate against current product evidence before recommending a promise.

## Draft after the direction is selected

For a chosen outbound play, prepare a concise example: relevant situation, specific problem, defensible value, and one low-friction next step. Personalization must come from a real observation; do not imply prior contact or claim to have inspected material that was inaccessible. A factual hiring announcement can support a question about workflow, not a claim that the recipient needs the product.

If proposing follow-ups, give a reason, bounded attempt count and spacing as a proposal. Stop on opt-out, negative response, incompatible fit, or an unresolved earlier send; replies move to a human conversation. No sequence is started by drafting it. Do not recommend multi-contact enterprise tactics for a low-value self-serve buyer without a reason tied to economics.

Check current channel/provider rules before recommending an execution route. A configured transactional mail provider is not automatically suitable for cold outreach. Keep this check proportional to an actual selected channel; research and ICP work do not require a sending stack.

## Derive other useful outputs

- **Social content:** turn a researched pain, objection, practical method, or founder observation into candidate ideas; use [editorial-review.md](editorial-review.md) for chosen drafts. The founder publishes in their own tool.
- **Reddit/community participation:** verify the exact discussion and community rules, explain the useful contribution, and disclose relevant affiliation when needed. A community is not a generic promotion slot.
- **Ads:** prepare the audience, promise, creative angle, destination, measurement, and proposed test cap. Label forecasts as hypotheses. No launch or spend occurs here.
- **Directories:** research audience relevance, eligibility, submission rules/cost, and the listing angle. A suggested listing is not a submission or acquisition result.

Use campaign-level observations to evaluate the play; do not force replies or conversions into social-post analytics. Follow [learning-loop.md](learning-loop.md). When the project names a lead adapter, record the play label on imported prospects and the qualification evidence as notes ([lead-adapter.md](lead-adapter.md)). If a founder later asks for another CRM/panel handoff, map only supported fields through an explicit, project-scoped import; producing research does not establish an integration or authorize application changes.
