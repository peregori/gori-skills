# Weekly GTM session

Resume `project.md` and the latest run. Read the relevant research, candidate plays/ideas, and available outcomes. State the dates, goal, founder-time limit, spending situation, and current stage. Do not demand every input or run every channel. A first launch run needs strategy and research; an established run needs updated evidence and decisions.

## Review and choose the work

Use [learning-loop.md](learning-loop.md) on available results. If nothing was executed, say so and identify what prevented it; do not infer market rejection from an empty results file. Import configured social analytics only when relevant to published posts. Batch requests for missing founder inputs; continue independent research.

Choose the current bottleneck: unclear ICP, weak product fit, poor source coverage, missing timing evidence, an unconvincing offer, insufficient reach, or conversion after interest. Recommend one coherent focus and a small set of deliverables within the founder's available time. Avoid converting a time budget into a mandatory daily routine or a quota for every channel.

## Research and prepare decisions

- For initial positioning or a changed hypothesis, use [strategy.md](strategy.md). Compare plausible segments and propose qualification criteria.
- For prospect/source work, use [market-research.md](market-research.md). Refresh relevant observations and signals; do not reconstruct the entire market map on every run.
- For campaign ideas, use [outbound-plays.md](outbound-plays.md). Present the strongest segment/signal/offer combinations and their evidence, uncertainty, effort, cost, and measurable next step.

Do the research and synthesis before asking the founder to choose. Make a recommendation and explain tradeoffs; do not pass back a generic strategy questionnaire or a long unranked list. Distinguish necessary founder decisions from details the agent can investigate. Already selected directions do not need repeat approval to prepare material.

## Content when useful

Derive candidate angles from audience pain, practical methods, objections, founder opinions, proof, public conversations, and product work. Product changes are sources of ideas, not proof of live capabilities. Keep useful founder observations that do not fit a campaign; do not make every post an outbound pitch or release note.

For each candidate give the audience problem, one point, evidence/source/date, why it matters, potential channel treatment, and missing proof. Compare with recent ideas to avoid repetition. Let the founder select angles before drafting a whole batch; a direct request to draft a particular angle already supplies that choice.

Use [editorial-review.md](editorial-review.md) for selected drafts and attach its quality verdict. Writing samples establish voice, not a quality ceiling. Test external techniques in original copy when relevant, without copying a creator's persona or treating their results as a forecast.

- **LinkedIn:** develop one useful argument or lesson with enough context to stand alone.
- **X:** keep a short point short; use a thread only when each part advances the idea.
- **Reddit:** verify the named community's current rules and the actual discussion. Explain the useful contribution and let the founder participate; no generic reply quota.
- **Other channels:** use only those relevant to the project and current run. Do not automatically cross-post or invent a cadence.

Propose timing only if useful for preparation; do not claim an optimal hour without evidence. Leave weak slots open. The founder uses their chosen publishing tool; scheduling is not part of this skill's default run.

## End with an executable handoff

Return a short weekly brief: what changed in our understanding, recommended/selected plays, prepared material, expected founder effort, known/proposed costs, and the measurement/review window. Name unresolved launch, offer, source, or execution prerequisites beside the affected material. Preparation can proceed while a product is unfinished; external-facing promises cannot assume blockers have cleared.

Save evidence and decisions in the existing workspace. Preserve declined ideas and reasons. Record what was actually executed only when supported by a source or founder report. Do not create tasks/cards, send messages, spend, submit listings, or establish recurring jobs merely because a weekly plan mentions them. End with one exact next action for the next requested session.
