# ICP and market strategy

Start from the project brief, existing evidence, and founder constraints. Research before asking the founder to supply answers that public sources or existing project material can establish. Ask about decisions, access, and firsthand experience that cannot be inferred. Do not require an offer or launch date to begin market research; mark those fields unresolved and avoid depending on them.

## Define a testable ICP

Translate a broad audience into distinct buying situations. Separate the account or individual who would pay, the person who uses the product, and an approver only where those roles actually differ. A solo creator need not be forced into company-headcount criteria; an agency's workflows may introduce requirements a solo user does not have.

Build a compact matrix with one row per segment:

| Field | Decision it supports |
| --- | --- |
| Segment and user/buyer | Who experiences the problem and who can choose a solution? |
| Job, pain, frequency | What recurring task is costly enough to change? |
| Current workaround | What would the product replace or supplement? |
| Required capabilities and proof | Can the product satisfy this use case today? What remains unknown? |
| Observable fit criteria | What public or permitted evidence would qualify a prospect? |
| Exclusions | What known condition makes this a poor or unsupported fit? |
| Timing signals | What dated event or expressed need could make action relevant now? |
| Reachable sources and channels | Where can this segment actually be found and reached? |
| Offer and first useful outcome | What next step delivers value and tests the hypothesis? |
| Evidence and status | Source/date; founder-selected hypothesis, observed evidence, or unverified assumption. |

Include evidence against the hypothesis. Avoid treating a founder's preference, an AI classification, or a few public complaints as validated demand or willingness to pay. Geography, language, price tolerance, and buying role are project choices or hypotheses, never universal defaults.

Recommend which segment to investigate first, explaining product fit, observable pain, source accessibility, expected founder effort, and acquisition economics. Compare a simpler alternative. Keep alternatives available without researching every possible segment equally. Founder agreement selects a test; it does not validate the market.

## Map the reachable market

Identify sources appropriate to the segment: public business directories, professional associations, community discussions, company sites, relevant job listings, creator portfolios, or permitted existing data. Record what each source covers, which fields it can supply, its access limits, likely bias, freshness, and cost. Proposed sources remain unverified until inspected.

Use complementary sources when they answer different questions or reduce a known coverage gap. Do not buy several databases merely to imitate a demonstration. Start with a bounded research sample appropriate to available time; report sample coverage and reasons for sampling. Call it a reachable prospect universe or sample. A scraped list is not a defensible total addressable market estimate.

## Define qualification before enrichment

Choose only criteria that affect an action and can plausibly be observed. Firmographics, expressed need, technology/workflow, and organizational structure can organize B2B criteria; they are optional lenses rather than mandatory score columns.

For each criterion record: definition, source, acceptable values including unknown, exclusion versus preference, and decision rationale. Funding, revenue, company size, follower counts, or a technology mention should not become hard requirements without product-specific justification and trustworthy data.

Start with explainable fit categories. If numeric weights or tier thresholds are useful, record the rule version and rationale, check representative positive/negative/unknown cases, and label the score uncalibrated until outcomes support it. An 80/100 score is not an 80% purchase probability. Use deterministic comparisons for known structured facts; use AI for research synthesis and ambiguous classification, retaining source evidence and an abstain option.

Save the current ICP matrix, source map, rules, and unresolved decisions in `project.md`. Preserve the evidence behind significant changes in the run. Proceed to [market-research.md](market-research.md) for a bounded application of those rules and [outbound-plays.md](outbound-plays.md) for campaign design.
