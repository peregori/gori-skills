# Editorial review

Writing quality is a separate pass after idea selection. The goal is a post the founder would plausibly publish, with one useful point and no claim the evidence cannot support.

## Learn the founder's writing

Read the founder's own published posts, comments, drafts, and accepted edits in `style.md`. Note point of view, sentence rhythm, vocabulary, directness, humor, structure, and recurring cuts. Cite the examples behind a proposed preference. These samples show how the founder has written, not the best they can write. Keep a recognizable point of view while improving weak openings, vague promises, repetition, or buried takeaways. Do not reuse sample sentences verbatim unless the founder chose that text for adaptation. If there are no own samples, say the draft is exploratory and ask for examples or edits; do not announce that you have learned the founder's voice.

Study named creators' original posts and current platform guidance when available. Extract a transferable mechanism such as a clear reader question, a concrete tension, a product tradeoff, or a useful example. Record the source URL/date and any visible performance evidence; distinguish measured results from a creator's claim or popularity. Their audience, reach, and goals differ from the founder's. Choose at most one structural technique to test in a draft and explain why it serves that idea. Write fresh language around the founder's actual experience. Do not reproduce a creator's phrasing, signature format, or persona.

## Edit each selected draft

Check these questions in order:

1. **Point:** Can a reader say what this post argues or teaches in one sentence? Cut secondary ideas.
2. **Truth:** Is every product, customer, result, and numerical claim supported and current? Recast an unshipped feature as a decision or lesson if useful.
3. **Specificity:** Is there a concrete observation, example, useful step, or genuine tension? Replace broad claims and filler.
4. **Founder fit:** Does the post preserve the founder's viewpoint and a tone they can stand behind while improving on older habits? Remove generic AI phrases, forced drama, repeated sentence patterns, and any copied creator style.
5. **Channel fit:** Does the form fit the selected platform and, for Reddit, the named community? A short idea need not become a thread; a LinkedIn post need not be a padded X post.
6. **Conversation:** Is there something a relevant reader could add, challenge, or ask about? A narrow, sincere question can help; do not append an empty “Thoughts?” or engagement bait.

Revise once against the strongest issue. If the post still lacks a point, proof, or founder fit, mark the slot open and name the missing input. A cadence target never overrides this verdict.

Attach a compact record to each draft:

```markdown
Quality verdict: ready | revise | open slot
Reason: [one concrete editorial reason]
Evidence checked: [source/date or missing proof]
Style checked against: [founder's own sample or accepted edit, or "no sample yet"]
Technique tested: [source and mechanism, or "none"]
```

Preserve the proposed and founder-edited final text in the run file when available. The difference becomes evidence for the next review, not an automatic new style rule.
